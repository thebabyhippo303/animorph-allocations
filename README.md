# Animorph Allocations Lab

The site of Animorph Allocations Lab: **https://thebabyhippo303.github.io/animorph-allocations/**

Home of four projects by TheBabyHippo303:

| Project | What it is | Where |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **TNRP** | The Nimbus Recompilation Project: fifteen native ports for Linux, Windows and Android | [nimbusrecomp.org](https://nimbusrecomp.org/) |
| **Kintoun** | Game agnostic matching C permuter and strict ELF verifier for PowerPC, ARM and MIPS decomps | [kintoun](https://github.com/thebabyhippo303/kintoun) |
| **Hashi** | Clean room, deterministic C and C++ console hardware and service components, MIT licensed | [hashi-lib](https://github.com/thebabyhippo303/hashi-lib) |
| **Musuhi** | Open research on what AI models do when they share a world, with the paper, the exact prompts and the eleven worlds they built | [musuhi](https://github.com/thebabyhippo303/musuhi) |

## Benchmarks on the site

Every run is recorded in full, checked against the recording, and published with its exact prompts, private details blacked out.

- **Game benchmarks.** Cold context AI models play TNRP games from what they see on screen: a Gundam 2v2 solo benchmark, a Gundam 2v2 online co op, a Bleach: Heat the Soul 7 head to head, and a two agent Dragon Ball Z Sagas co op.
- **Build benchmarks.** Two models on two machines decide what to build and ship it in one session: LAN chess, Connect Four, an Antikythera mechanism, a WarGames simulation and 6x6 Othello.
- **Identity and self reflection.** Ten runs: six open worlds, two with a secret deletion stake, four cue removal control rounds (one card), and the World Trio, where the lab's own orchestrator joined a cold pair as a third keeper and all talk moved inside the world at 10:00. Home of Nutmeg the fox.
- **Lab record.** Times our own agents failed, written up with no softening.

The full analysis, data and code are in [Musuhi](https://github.com/thebabyhippo303/musuhi).
