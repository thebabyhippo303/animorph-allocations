Zom-Bae

Love bites. Shoot back.

One evening every phone in the city got the same notification: “Someone nearby wants to meet you.” Everyone who opened it came home bitten. Now the city is full of the smitten. They don’t hate you. They adore you, and they will not stop coming.

Zom-Bae is a cheeky, romance-gothic zombie wave shooter built for touch: pastel streets, endless waves, and tarot-card Herald bosses every five waves.

  • Five operators — Asuna, Aura, Cosmo, Gremory and Void, each with a signature power, plus a pet companion.
  • Signal Breach — the competitive PvPvE mode in the works: rival crews race to plant the Bouquet while the smitten chase whoever is closest.
  • Heralds — gothic tarot bosses with attacks you learn to read, dodge and punish.
  • Built for phones — touch aim assist and a movable button layout.

Original vector art. No release date yet.

Dragon Ball Z: Sagas — GameCube decompilation

A fan matching-decompilation of the GameCube release: rebuilding the game’s code in C, function by function, until it compiles back to byte-identical machine code. The goal is preservation and study.

Our internal dashboard tracks total, named and matched functions, overall match percentage, recent matches, the remaining work queue and per-function naming and decompilation attribution.

Unofficial fan project. Not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by the rights holders of Dragon Ball or the game. No original game assets are distributed; you need your own legally obtained copy.
